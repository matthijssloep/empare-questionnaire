    Placeholder. The privacy notice, the controller for respondent data and the retention period are to be set by the organisation that runs the survey (open decision R1-9).

    Design commitments from the concept: the main dataset contains no names or contact details of respondents; the registry name is optional and used only for de-duplication; contact details for interviews are collected in a separate form that is not linked to the answers; country results are reported only above a minimum number of responses.
